Privacy Policy for Fitness AI Agent

Effective date: July 7, 2026

Fitness AI Agent is a personal fitness tracking assistant that receives WhatsApp messages from users who choose to interact with it.

Information We Collect

  • WhatsApp phone number
  • User-provided messages
  • Meal descriptions
  • Meal images, when sent by the user
  • Workout logs
  • Body weight logs
  • Fitness goals and preferences

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide meal tracking, workout tracking, nutrition analysis, body weight tracking, goal tracking, and fitness coaching summaries.

Data Storage

User data may be stored in the application database and local storage controlled by the app owner.

Data Sharing

Fitness AI Agent does not sell user data. User data is not shared with advertisers.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their data by following the instructions on our Data Deletion Instructions page.
